HISTORICAL INCIDENT (NOT AN EXPERIMENTAL TEST)

25 Years Ago: Fire Aboard Space Station Mir (February 23, 1997)

NASA History Division · SP-4225 "The Mir Fire: Operational Emergency in Orbit"

A solid-fuel oxygen-generation canister (Vika / TGK) ignited in the Kvant-1 module, producing an open blowtorch flame, toxic dense smoke, and blocking the Soyuz evacuation path. The crew battled the fire in zero buoyancy with multiple extinguishers.

Key Safety Takeaway: In microgravity, smoke does not buoyant-settle; toxic aerosols distribute uniformly through ventilation loops, proving that Earth-calibrated smoke detectors and manual suppression require specialized zero-gravity validation.

NASA SAFETY STANDARD

NASA-STD-6001: Flammability, Offgassing, and Compatibility Standard

NASA Technical Standards Program

Defines NASA Test 1 (upward flame propagation in 1G at maximum expected operational oxygen concentrations). Materials must self-extinguish within 6 inches to pass.

Scientific Boundary: Microgravity research (such as BASS-II and SAFFIRE) revealed that some materials passing 1G Test 1 can burn steadily under gentle ventilation in freefall due to reduced radiative and convective heat losses.

METHOD & LIMITATIONS

How We Use the Data

A transparent disclosure of project provenance, data modeling, and operational boundaries.

1. Data Provenance & Scope

All 23 investigation records reflect high-level experiment overviews and documented operational test matrices from the NASA PSI repository. They do not represent continuous millisecond sensor telemetry.

2. Transparent Handling of Missing Values

When an investigation summary lacks an explicit oxygen level, chamber pressure, or airflow rate, the field is explicitly marked Not available. Freefall Explorer never fabricates or synthesizes missing experimental values.

3. Range Semantics & Interpretation

A documented parameter range (e.g., 16%–40% vol O₂) denotes that the investigation's test envelope spanned those conditions, not that every intermediate concentration was tested or resulted in flame propagation.

4. Simulation & SI Chat Boundaries

The 3D WebGL combustion chamber and SI assistant provide interactive, rule-based representations of zero-buoyancy physics for educational exploration. They are research tools, not certified flight fire-safety prediction engines.
