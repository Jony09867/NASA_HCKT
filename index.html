COMBUSTION PHYSICS

Understand fire behavior

Buoyancy-driven convection ceases in freefall. Flames become rounded, soot accumulates, and low-speed ventilation can sustain steady combustion where Earth fires self-extinguish. Investigated by BASS-II (PSI-25) and SAFFIRE-I (PSI-98).

SMOKE DETECTION

Improve smoke detection

In zero gravity, smoke particles do not rise. Aerosol droplets remain suspended in cabin airflows without settling, requiring specialized sensors calibrated to size distribution rather than buoyancy. Investigated by SAME-R (PSI-101) and SAME (PSI-102).

MISSION SAFETY

Learn from past incidents

The 1997 Mir oxygen-generator solid-fuel fire underscored the urgent need for verified material flammability limits and gaseous suppression, bridging NASA-STD-6001 Test 1 to microgravity exploration. Investigated by BASS (PSI-26).
